Dynamic Chemical Safety Monitoring for Process SMEs

ChemSentry connects physical chemical store room sensors with supplier Safety Data Sheets (SDSs). No safety thresholds are hardcoded — every limit is dynamically retrieved, reconciled, and cited from source documents at query time.

📖 Classical IR Retrieval

Position-aware inverted index, k-gram & Levenshtein tolerant matching, and TF-IDF cosine ranking extract exact safety limits directly from PDF manuals.

🔀 Supplier Conflict Detection

Reverse Jaccard duplicate detection flags when two suppliers issue conflicting storage temperature ceilings for the same substance.

🛡️ Human-in-the-Loop

Deterministic SAFE / WARNING verdicts route straight to qualified Safety Officers for sign-off before alerts can be dismissed.
